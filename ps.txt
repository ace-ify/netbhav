PS-02: Mandi Opportunity Agent

PROBLEM
Farmers often have to decide where to sell their produce using fragmented mandi price information. A higher market price may not result in higher earnings once transportation and distance are considered.

CHALLENGE
Build an AI-assisted decision-support tool that takes a farmer's crop, quantity, and location, analyzes market-price information from multiple sources, and identifies potential selling opportunities.

Example: Crop + quantity + location → nearby mandi prices → transport estimation → net realization → opportunity shortlist.

MVP / EXPECTED OUTCOME
- Farmer/crop profile
- At least one market-price data source
- Nearby mandi comparison
- Transport-cost calculation
- Net realization comparison
- Clear opportunity recommendation

BONUS
- Historical price trends
- Multiple data sources
- Map visualization
- FPO/bulk-selling mode
- Hindi/Indic language support
